Two strategic observations now that this is shipped:
The fact that Sally co-builds the working set with the AI is doing more strategic work than I initially gave it credit for. It changes the buyer psychology from "I'm being sold to by an algorithm" to "I'm researching with a smart collaborator." This is the same psychological shift that made Perplexity feel different from Google — search is transactional, exploration is relational. The session-pool concept is what makes WeCater feel relational rather than transactional.
The Tier 3 design has one open question I want to flag for v2 even though MVP is fine. Right now Tony's Italian Kitchen has an estimated 5X "platform-average" earn rate. In production, we should consider letting Sally see what she'd earn if she included her Welcome 2X stack — "if Tony's activates AND your Welcome 2X is still live, you'd earn ~1,860 Bites." That projection makes the request-quote action even stickier because Sally is now invested in seeing the activation happen. Just a note for the post-MVP backlog.
